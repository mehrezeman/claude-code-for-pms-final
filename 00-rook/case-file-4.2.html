The mission: trouble call to buzzing phone

Twelve things actually happen between "something went wrong" and a specific person's phone going off. Tap any step to read it in plain English. Every step names the file it lives in.

Steps marked PART OF THE LOOP feed each other: a miss at step 11 drags down the number step 6 looks up next time, which drags down the score step 7 builds, which drags down the ranking step 8 produces — so the same responder is more likely to time out again at step 10, and round it goes.
