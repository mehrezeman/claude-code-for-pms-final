EXHIBIT B — THE STORY

What happened, plainly

4.2 shipped a long-requested, genuinely good change — weighting proximity higher so remote responders stopped losing jobs to someone with a better record 40 minutes away. It shipped alongside a smaller change: the answer window cut from 90 seconds to 60. Acceptance rate dropped. The outgoing PM's read: probably seasonal. It wasn't. Four responders nearly stopped getting called at all, and kept getting worse, not better, for weeks. The cause wasn't the headline change — it was a quiet bug in how Rook scores a responder's recent reliability: a miss costs more than a take earns back, and nothing ever lets that score recover on its own.
